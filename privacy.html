1. What the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, the game uses your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

2. Advertising (Google Ad Manager)

Blorbit offers optional rewarded video ads. You can choose to watch a short video in return for a reward:

  • in the shop, a free card pack;
  • on a locked galaxy, unlocking that galaxy early instead of waking its friends first.

You never have to watch one, and saying no doesn't affect the rest of the game: every galaxy can still be unlocked for free by playing. These ads are provided by Google through Google Ad Manager.

To have a video ready when you ask for one, the game loads Google's ad code and may request an ad in the background while you play. When it does, Google and its advertising partners (third-party vendors) may:

  • use cookies, similar technologies (such as device identifiers and local storage) and information such as your IP address, browser and device type;
  • use that information to deliver ads, measure how they perform, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show personalised ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Your ad choices

  • In the game: if privacy rules where you live give you consent or opt-out choices, Settings → Privacy & cookie settings lets you review or change them at any time.
  • With Google: you can turn off personalised ads from Google in My Ad Center.
  • Other vendors: you can opt out of personalised advertising from many third-party vendors at aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (Europe).
  • Blocking cookies in your browser or using an ad blocker also stops these ads. The rest of the game still works; only the free video packs won't be available.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you'll see a consent message from Google's certified consent tool before any ad cookies are used for personalised ads. We rely on your consent for this. You can withdraw it at any time through Settings → Privacy & cookie settings. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything done before.

US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with consumer privacy laws (such as California), you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Where this applies, a message from Google's consent tool lets you opt out, and you can revisit it through Settings → Privacy & cookie settings. We don't sell personal information for money.

3. Other services the site uses

Blorbit is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, contact us and we'll help remove it.
