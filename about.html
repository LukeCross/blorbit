About Blorbit

Blorbit is a cosy, relaxing game you play in your browser. You're a wobbly alien water blob, rolling around tiny grey planets. Everywhere you roll you leave a trail of slime, and the slime brings the world back to life. There are no timers and no rush: take as long as you like.

▶ Play Blorbit

How to play

  • Roll and slime. Roll over the dead grey ground. Your slime trail fades into grass, flowers, trees, ponds and critters.
  • Clear the spots. Every biome is dotted with little named spots, like a flower bed, an old dirt road, an oasis or a frozen lake. Slime enough of one to clear it, with a happy chime and a chance at a collectible card.
  • Restore biomes. Clear every spot in a biome and the whole biome blooms. Its sleeping creature wakes up and becomes a new skin for your blob.
  • Finish the planet. Each planet has four biomes. Restore them all, then warp to a brand-new planet or keep rolling around the one you saved.
  • Pick up stardust. Sparkly stardust trails cover every planet. Spend it in the shop on card packs.

Collecting

There are 360 cards to collect, eight in every biome: three common, two uncommon, one rare, one epic and one legendary. You find them by clearing spots, or by opening card packs in the shop. Card packs, Shiny packs and Prism packs each have better odds than the last, and every pack promises at least one card of a certain rarity. Your collection book keeps track of everything you've found, and every creature you wake is a new skin for your blob to wear.

Controls

On a computerW A S D or the arrow keys to roll and steer. R auto-roll, B collection book, P shop, G galaxies, N new planet, M mute, number keys to change skins.
On a phone or tabletYour blob rolls by itself: hold the left or right side of the screen to turn. Prefer a joystick? Switch in Settings → Touch controls.

Settings also has graphics quality, turning sensitivity and a colorblind mode.

Free to play

Blorbit is free. If you like, you can watch an optional short video for a free card pack, or to unlock a galaxy early. You never have to: everything can be unlocked by playing.
